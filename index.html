WELCOME TO THE MYSTERY

CASE #001

THE LOST ROOM

Some doors are better left unopened...

LEVEL 01 — THE AWAKENING

You wake up in a completely dark room.

You don't remember how you got here.

There is a door in front of you. It has no handle.

On the wall someone has written:

"YOU ALREADY HAVE THE KEY."

Think about what you are using right now...


🕯️
LEVEL 01 COMPLETE

The door opens...

But there is no hallway.

LEVEL 02 — THE SILENT ROOM

You enter another room.

It looks exactly like the first.

You take one step.

Step...

Another sound answers.

Step...

You stop.

The other footsteps don't.

What follows sound when nothing is visible?


👁️
LEVEL 02 COMPLETE

The footsteps suddenly stop.

Something whispers your name.

LEVEL 03 — THE HIDDEN TRUTH

A small light appears.

On the table lies an old photograph.

The room in the photograph is the same room you are standing in.

But the photograph was taken years ago.

"I WAS HERE BEFORE YOU."

What object holds this memory?


🪞
LEVEL 03 COMPLETE

You look closer at the photograph.

The person inside it...

Looks like you.

LEVEL 04 — THE FINAL DOOR

The photograph suddenly disappears.

A final door appears.

This time, there is a handle.

Above the door is one final message:

"THE THING YOU LOST WAS NEVER OUTSIDE."

You remember the room.

You remember the photograph.

You remember yourself.

What was really lost?


☠️

THE FINAL TRUTH

The door opens.

There is nothing behind it.

Only darkness.

You look at the photograph one last time.

This time you notice something.

The person in the photograph is YOU.

And the date on the photograph...

IS TOMORROW.

The room was never a prison.

It was a test.

Every clue. Every door. Every sound.

Was leading you here.

YOU WERE NEVER LOST.

YOU WERE BEING LED HERE.

CASE #001 — CLOSED
